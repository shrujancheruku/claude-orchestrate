# Evidence ledger

The collated findings of the research behind map #588: every Implementable finding from the ten area notes and the three oversight-strand notes in this folder, each with a stable id, an evidence grade and its source, followed by the union of the candidate orchestration paradigms those findings back. The paradigm grilling (#613) and the claim language (#614) work from this file; the per-area notes hold the reading, the numbers in context and the open questions. Full references are in [`docs/sources.md`](../sources.md).

## How to read it

Each row is one finding. The **id** is stable: later tickets, ADRs and docs cite it, so a row is never renumbered, and a superseded row is struck through rather than deleted. The **for orchestrate** column says how the finding would be used: `default` (a step every run takes), `flag` (an opt-in paradigm), `measure` (something orchestrate should record about itself), `rule` (a constraint on a design) or `reject`.

| grade | means |
|---|---|
| A | A controlled study or measurement with an effect size: a randomised or matched comparison, a preregistered trial, or a large field measurement with a stated comparison group. |
| B | A benchmark result or an ablation without controls for confounds: one benchmark, one harness, or one model family. |
| C | An industry report, a vendor page, or one system's field observation. |
| D | A field observation from the Human Oversight System (HOS), or a paper read only through HOS. Used in the oversight-strand rows. |

A grade like `A / C` means the finding has two parts graded separately, as the row says. A source marked `(secondary)` or `(via HOS, not read)` was not read at its primary source; see [Thin evidence](#thin-evidence).

**The honesty rule.** A default step must cite an A or B row that measured that step on coding agents. C and D rows may motivate a flag or a measurement, never a default. A paradigm's best grade below is the best grade among its backing rows; it says how strong the strongest evidence is, not that every part of the paradigm is backed at that grade.
